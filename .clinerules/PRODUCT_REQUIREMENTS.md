# Product Requirements Document (High-Level)

This document outlines the vision, target user, and feature set for the Basketball Training App.

## 1. App Philosophy & Vision

The core vision is to **bridge the gap between physical skill execution and in-game cognitive decision-making.**

Most solo training focuses on perfecting the *how* (e.g., shooting form), but neglects the *why* and *when* (e.g., deciding to shoot vs. drive based on a stimulus). This app aims to be the first true solo training partner that trains the entire perception-action loop, making practice more effective and game-like.

## 2. Target User

A dedicated basketball player of any level (from high school to adult league) who is motivated to improve through individual practice. They are looking for structure, data-driven feedback, and a way to practice the mental aspects of the game when they don't have a coach or training partner.

## 3. Core Features

### MVP (Phase 1) Features

- **Pre-defined Workout Library:** A small set of high-quality, foundational workouts.
- **Active Workout Engine:** A user interface to guide the player through drills with timers and instructions.
- **Manual Data Logging:** Ability for users to input makes, misses, and other stats for each drill.
- **Local Workout History:** A log of completed workouts and their results, stored on the device.
- **Wellness/Injury Logger:** A simple post-workout prompt to log physical feeling.

### Future Features Roadmap

- **AI-Powered Shot Tracking:** Use the device's camera to automatically track makes and misses.
- **Custom Workout Builder:** Allow users to create and save their own workout routines from a library of drills.
- **LLM-Powered AI Coach:** A conversational interface (using Gemini) to dynamically generate personalized workouts and provide post-session feedback.
- **Advanced Analytics:** Detailed performance charts, shot-zone analysis, and wellness trend correlation.
- **Team/Coach Functionality:** A plan for coaches to assign workouts and track the progress of multiple players.
